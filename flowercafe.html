Our Story

About The Flowershop Cafe

A unique concept bringing together the beauty of flowers and the art of fine dining.

A Fresh Idea for Lagos

The Flowershop Cafe introduces a fresh idea to Lagos, seamlessly blending a flower shop with a cozy cafe under one roof. As you enter, a sense of tranquility envelops you, offering respite from the busy streets of Victoria Island.

Our establishment harmonizes fresh offerings with a serene atmosphere, crafting a vibrant hub in the downtown of Lagos. Whether you are here for a morning coffee, a business lunch, or to pick up a beautiful bouquet, we promise an experience like no other.

"Everything we do is a matter of heart, body and soul"

— Malaika Najem, Co-Founder
